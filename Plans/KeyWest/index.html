Miami Beach Highlights

Culture

Art Deco Historic District

Ocean Drive between 5th-15th Streets. 800+ preserved buildings from the 1930s-40s. Free walking tour or self-guided.

Art

Wynwood Walls

2520 NW 2nd Avenue. Outdoor museum of street art. Instagram-worthy murals by world-renowned artists. Free entry.

Food

Little Havana

SW 8th Street (Calle Ocho). Cuban culture hub. Versailles Restaurant (3555 SW 8th St), cigar shops, domino park. 20 min drive.

Shopping

Lincoln Road Mall

Between Alton Rd & Washington Ave. Pedestrian outdoor shopping district. Boutiques, cafes, street performers.

🎒 Packing Essentials

  • Reef-safe sunscreen - SPF 50 (Keys & Miami sun is intense)
  • Beach attire - Swimsuits, cover-ups, flip-flops
  • Snorkel mask - Optional, provided on tours
  • Water shoes - For rocky beach entries
  • Light jacket - Cold fronts can drop temps to 60s
  • Nice dinner outfit - For Miami Beach restaurants

💡 Pro Tips

  • Gas up in Marathon and Key West (limited stations between)
  • Bahia Honda fills early on weekends - arrive by 10 AM
  • Key West parking is $4-5/hr - use hotel parking and walk
  • Cash for El Siboney (Cuban restaurant, cash only)
  • Bring water bottle - Free refills on Dry Tortugas ferry
