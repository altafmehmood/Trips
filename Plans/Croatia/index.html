🗺️ Your Route: Dubrovnik to Zagreb

A reverse south-to-north Croatia road trip combining coastal beauty, nature, and culture.

🏰
Dubrovnik
Days 2-4 • Pearl of Adriatic

Start in the magnificent walled city. Walk the famous city walls, explore Game of Thrones locations, and hike Mount Srđ for panoramic views.

City Walls 2km Mount Srđ Hike Old Town UNESCO
🏛️
Split & Hvar
Days 5-6 • Palace & Island

Explore the 1,700-year-old Diocletian's Palace in Split, then take a ferry to stunning Hvar Island with its lavender fields and crystal waters.

Diocletian's Palace Hvar Island Ferry Adriatic Paradise
🌊
Krka & Zadar
Days 7-8 • Waterfalls & Coast

Visit Krka National Park's spectacular Skradinski Buk waterfall, then experience Zadar's unique Sea Organ and world-famous sunset.

Krka Waterfalls Sea Organ Greeting to Sun
🏞️
Plitvice Lakes
Day 9 • UNESCO Heritage

Full day at Croatia's most famous national park with 16 interconnected turquoise lakes and spectacular waterfalls. Overnight at resort just 0.5km from park entrance.

Trail C or K Veliki Slap 78m Turquoise Lakes
🇸🇮
Lake Bled & Zagreb
Day 10 • Alpine Finale

Early drive to Slovenia's fairy-tale Lake Bled, then continue to Zagreb Airport. Final morning with Bled's iconic island church and mountain views.

Bled Island Alpine Scenery Return to Zagreb

🏨 Hotels to Book

Sun Gardens Dubrovnik

📍 Na moru 1, 20235 Orašac, Croatia

Check-in: April 25 • Check-out: April 27 (2 nights)

Five-star Leading Hotels of the World resort on the Adriatic coast with natural pebble beach, 4 pools (1 indoor), award-winning 3,000 sqm spa, 16 restaurants and bars, kids club, tennis courts, and complimentary shuttle to Old Town (15 min). Rated #1 in area on TripAdvisor.

Boutique Hotel Luxe

📍 Ul. kralja Zvonimira 6, 21000 Split, Croatia

Check-in: April 27 • Check-out: April 30 (3 nights)

Contemporary design hotel just 5 minutes from Diocletian's Palace with 30 modern rooms, many featuring sea views and private balconies. Includes spa, hot tub, sauna, fitness center, cigar lounge, and complimentary welcome wine. Perfect base for exploring Split and day trip to Hvar Island. Guests love the helpful staff and rich breakfast.

Heritage Hotel Bastion

📍 Bedemi Zadarskih Pobuna 13, 23000 Zadar, Croatia

Check-in: April 30 • Check-out: May 1 (1 night)

Relais & Châteaux boutique hotel in a 13th-century fortress in Zadar's historic core. Features handmade art-deco furniture, Castello Spa & Wellness Center, Michelin-star restaurant, and individually decorated rooms with exposed stone walls. Walking distance to Sea Organ.

Fenomen Plitvice

📍 Plitvica Selo 81, 53231 Plitvicka Jezera, Croatia

Check-in: May 1 • Check-out: May 2 (1 night)

Rated #1 resort near Plitvice Lakes with 18 luxury cabins on the edge of the UNESCO park. Just 0.5km from Veliki Slap waterfall with private jacuzzi, sauna, archery, cycling, and free breakfast on terrace. Couples rate the location 9.5/10.

Hotel Triglav Bled

📍 Kolodvorska cesta 33, 4260 Bled, Slovenia

Check-in: May 2 • Check-out: May 3 (1 night)

Historic 4-star hotel from 1906, reopened in 2009 after renovations. Located 650m from Lake Bled with 21 eco-friendly rooms, most with lake-view balconies. Features indoor pool, spa with massages and aromatherapy, award-winning à la carte restaurant 1906 Bled with Slovene Nouvelle cuisine, piano bar, and wine cellar. Early checkout (5:30 AM) for morning drive to Zagreb and 10:40 AM flight.

Practical Information

🌤️ Weather in Late April/Early May

Average Temperature: 15-20°C (59-68°F)

Perfect spring weather with fewer crowds and high water levels at waterfalls from melting snow - ideal for hiking!

Coastal areas (Dubrovnik, Split, Zadar): Warm and sunny - perfect start to your trip!

Lake Bled: Can be cooler in the Alps - bring layers for mornings and evenings at the end of your journey
