🏛️ Split & Hvar

Days 4-5 • Ancient Roman Palace & Island Paradise • April 27-28, 2026

Overview

Explore Split's 1,700-year-old Diocletian's Palace, then take a ferry to stunning Hvar Island with its lavender fields, crystal waters, and historic town

Day 4 - Monday, April 27
Dubrovnik → Split

🚗 Checkout Dubrovnik & Pick Up Rental Car

  • Checkout from Sun Gardens Dubrovnik
  • Pick up rental car at Dubrovnik City Center location
  • 7-day one-way rental to Zagreb (returning May 3)

🛣️ Scenic Drive to Split

  • 3-hour coastal drive (230km)
  • Drive along stunning Adriatic coastline
  • Pass through small coastal towns
  • Views of islands and turquoise waters

🏨 Afternoon Arrival in Split

  • Check into Boutique Hotel Luxe Split
  • 5 minutes from Diocletian's Palace
  • Evening stroll through palace and Riva promenade
  • Dinner at local konoba
Day 5 - Tuesday, April 28
Hvar Island Day Trip

⛴️ Fast Ferry to Hvar Island

  • Multiple daily ferries from Split port (50 minutes - 1 hour)
  • Jadrolinija catamaran - Fast, comfortable, reliable
  • Depart Split ~9 AM, arrive Hvar Town ~10 AM
  • Book tickets in advance during high season (online or at port)
  • Ferry drops you right in Hvar Town harbor

🏰 Hvar Town - Island Capital

  • Sunniest island in Croatia - Over 2,700 hours of sunshine annually!
  • Historic Venetian architecture meets vibrant Mediterranean culture
  • 13th-century city walls and fortifications
  • Elegant St. Stephen's Cathedral on main square
  • Charming narrow streets lined with lavender and rosemary

🏰 Fortica Fortress (Spanish Fort)

  • Best views on the island! - 360° panorama
  • 20-minute uphill walk from town (steep but worth it)
  • 16th-century Venetian fortress overlooking harbor
  • See Pakleni Islands archipelago spreading before you
  • Perfect spot for photos and sunset views
  • Small entrance fee (~€5)

🌿 Lavender Fields & Island Beauty

  • Hvar famous for lavender production since ancient times
  • Late April: Fields beginning to bloom with purple hues
  • Visit local lavender shops for oils, soaps, honey
  • Pine forests meet crystal-clear Adriatic waters
  • Mediterranean vegetation: rosemary, sage, wild herbs

🏖️ Pakleni Islands (Optional Boat Trip)

  • Archipelago of 21 small islands off Hvar's coast
  • Taxi boats depart from Hvar harbor (€10-15 round trip)
  • Palmižana - Most popular, beach restaurants
  • Mlini Beach - Secluded pebble beach
  • Crystal-clear turquoise water perfect for swimming
  • If time permits and weather's warm!

🍷 Hvar Wine & Dining

  • Local wines: Plavac Mali (red), Bogdanuša (white)
  • Fresh seafood caught daily - grilled fish, octopus salad
  • Gregada - traditional fisherman's stew
  • Lunch at harbor-side konoba with island views

⛴️ Return Ferry to Split

  • Late afternoon ferry back to Split (~5-6 PM)
  • Arrive Split evening
  • Dinner at Split's Riva promenade
  • Overnight at Boutique Hotel Luxe Split
💡 Split & Hvar Tips
Local Insights

Split Tips

  • Diocletian's Palace streets are free to wander
  • Pay only for Cathedral/bell tower (€6) and basement halls (€6)
  • Early morning (before 9 AM) best for photos without crowds
  • Avoid tourist trap restaurants on main Riva - explore side streets
  • Green Market near palace for fresh produce and local atmosphere

Hvar Ferry Tips

  • Book Jadrolinija catamaran tickets in advance online
  • Ferry terminal is 10-minute walk from Diocletian's Palace
  • Arrive 30 minutes before departure
  • Round-trip ticket €20-30 per person
  • Check return ferry schedule - last ferry usually ~7 PM

Hvar Island Tips

  • Fortica Fortress: Worth the climb - best views on island
  • Lavender products: Buy authentic from local shops, not tourist traps
  • Pakleni Islands: Only if time permits and weather is warm
  • Bring sunscreen and hat - very sunny island!

Photography

  • Split: Golden hour at Riva promenade with palm trees
  • Hvar: Fortica Fortress panoramic views over town and Pakleni Islands
  • Hvar harbor: Colorful fishing boats and Venetian architecture

Time Management

  • Ferry to Hvar: 50 min - 1 hour each way
  • Hvar Island: 5-6 hours exploring (10 AM - 5 PM)
  • Fortica Fortress: Allow 1.5 hours (walk up + explore)
  • Return to Split evening for dinner and overnight

Dining Recommendations

  • Hvar: Lunch at harbor konoba - grilled fish and octopus salad
  • Split: Konoba Matejuška (seafood), Fife (casual, local favorite)
  • Try Hvar wines: Plavac Mali, Bogdanuša
  • Split evening: Dinner on Riva or explore Varoš neighborhood
