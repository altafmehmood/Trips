🇸🇮 Lake Bled, Slovenia

Day 9 • Alpine Gem • May 2, 2026

Overview

Day visit to Slovenia's fairy-tale Lake Bled with its iconic island church, clifftop medieval castle, and stunning Alpine scenery before continuing to Zagreb

Day 9 - Saturday, May 2
Lake Bled → Zagreb

🌅 Morning at Lake Bled

  • Arrived previous evening from Plitvice (Day 8)
  • Checked into Hotel Triglav Bled
  • Full morning to explore Lake Bled
  • Start early to maximize time before Zagreb drive

🏝️ Bled Island (Blejski Otok)

  • The only island in Slovenia!
  • Take traditional pletna boat (€15-20 round trip) - iconic experience
  • Or rent your own rowboat to explore at your own pace
  • Church of the Assumption - climb 99 steps to the church
  • Ring the "wishing bell" - legend says your wish will come true
  • Beautiful Baroque church dating from 1698

🏰 Bled Castle (Blejski Grad)

  • Slovenia's oldest castle - perched 130m above the lake
  • Dating back to 1004 AD (over 1,000 years old!)
  • Steep climb but rewarded with breathtaking panoramic views
  • Museum showcasing castle and regional history
  • Castle restaurant with terrace overlooking the lake
  • Traditional printing press and wine cellar
  • Best views: from the upper courtyard terrace

🚶 Walk Around Lake Bled

  • 6km (3.7 miles) loop taking approximately 1.5 hours
  • Flat, easy walk suitable for all fitness levels
  • Different perspectives of the lake, island, and mountains
  • Photo opportunities at every turn
  • Pass by swimming areas, cafes, and viewpoints

📸 Ojstrica Viewpoint (Optional)

  • THE iconic Lake Bled photo spot
  • Short but steep 20-minute hike (400m elevation gain)
  • Aerial view of lake, island, castle, and Julian Alps
  • Best for sunset or early morning light
  • Classic postcard view of Lake Bled

🍰 Try Bled Cream Cake (Kremšnita)

  • Lake Bled's most famous dessert since 1953
  • Layers of vanilla custard cream and whipped cream between puff pastry
  • Best at Park Hotel or Hotel Triglav restaurants
  • Over 15 million served since inception!

🏞️ Vintgar Gorge (Optional)

  • 1.6km gorge with dramatic waterfalls and wooden walkways
  • 4 miles (15 min drive) from Lake Bled
  • Timed entry tickets must be purchased in advance online
  • Allow 1.5 hours for visit
  • Worth it if time permits!

🏨 Overnight in Lake Bled

  • Stay lakeside to enjoy evening and early morning serenity
  • Lake is magical at sunset and sunrise
  • Evening stroll along illuminated lakefront

🚗 Afternoon Drive to Zagreb

  • Checkout Lake Bled after lunch
  • 2-2.5 hour drive to Zagreb (140km)
  • Cross back into Croatia from Slovenia
  • Check into Canopy by Hilton Zagreb (near main train station)
  • Evening and next morning to explore Croatia's capital
  • No early morning rush - full night in Zagreb before 10:40 AM flight!
💡 Lake Bled Tips
Local Insights

Timing Recommendations

  • Start with pletna boat to island (before crowds around 9-10 AM)
  • Bled Castle mid-morning when it opens (8 AM in summer)
  • Lake walk in afternoon (any time is good)
  • Ojstrica viewpoint for sunset if weather is clear

Money & Practicalities

  • Slovenia uses Euro (€) - Croatia uses Kuna, so get some Euros
  • Bled Castle entrance: €13-15 adults
  • Pletna boat: €15-20 round trip (plus €6 church entry)
  • Parking: Several paid lots around the lake (€2-4/hour)

What to Eat

  • Kremšnita - Bled cream cake (must try!)
  • Potica - Traditional Slovenian nut roll
  • Fresh lake fish at lakeside restaurants
  • Try Slovenian wines - different from Croatian

Photography

  • Ojstrica viewpoint: Best for classic aerial shot
  • Mala Osojnica: Slightly higher, more dramatic (steeper hike)
  • From Bled Castle: Great for looking down on the island
  • Evening: Lake illuminations create beautiful reflections

Weather Note

  • April in the Alps can be cooler than Croatia coast
  • Bring layers - mornings/evenings can be chilly
  • Mountains may still have snow in April - beautiful backdrop!
