🏛️ Zagreb

Day 10 • Croatia's Vibrant Capital • May 3, 2026

Overview

Final stop in Croatia's historic capital city before departure - explore the charming Upper Town, vibrant markets, and café culture

Day 10 - Sunday, May 3
Zagreb & Return to Houston

🚗 Arrive from Lake Bled

  • Drive from Lake Bled to Zagreb (2-2.5 hours)
  • Return rental car at Zagreb airport or city center
  • Brief exploration time depending on flight schedule

🏛️ Upper Town (Gornji Grad)

  • St. Mark's Church - Famous for its colorful tiled roof depicting Croatian coat of arms
  • Zagreb Cathedral - Neo-Gothic twin spires visible from across the city
  • Lotrščak Tower - Climb for panoramic city views, cannon fires daily at noon
  • Walk through historic Upper Town streets with Austro-Hungarian architecture

🍽️ Dolac Market

  • Zagreb's famous outdoor farmers market since 1930
  • Red umbrellas, fresh produce, local delicacies
  • Try traditional Croatian street food

🚶 Tkalčićeva Street

  • Pedestrian street lined with colorful buildings
  • Vibrant cafes, restaurants, and bars
  • Perfect for people-watching and Croatian coffee culture

✈️ Departure

  • Return rental car at Zagreb location
  • Depart Zagreb on British Airways BA 849 to London Heathrow
  • Connect to British Airways BA 197 to Houston IAH
  • Arrive Houston same day (time zones work in your favor!)
  • End of an incredible Croatia adventure!
💡 Zagreb Tips
Local Insights

Getting Around

  • Upper Town is walkable from Lower Town via funicular or stairs
  • Efficient tram system for longer distances
  • Most attractions are within walking distance

Food Recommendations

  • Štrukli - Traditional Croatian pastry with cheese
  • Ćevapi - Grilled meat sausages
  • Try local Croatian wines

Accommodation

  • Canopy by Hilton Zagreb - City Centre - Modern hotel in prime location
  • Walking distance to Upper Town, Dolac Market, and main attractions
  • Stylish rooms with local Croatian design touches
  • Perfect base for exploring Zagreb on foot
