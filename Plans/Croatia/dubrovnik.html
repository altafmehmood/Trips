🏰 Dubrovnik

Days 2-4 • Pearl of the Adriatic • April 25-27, 2026

Overview

Explore the magnificent walled city of Dubrovnik, walk the iconic city walls, hike Mount Srđ, and discover Game of Thrones filming locations

Day 2 - Saturday, April 25
Arrive in Dubrovnik - Old Town

✈️ Arrival in Dubrovnik

  • Arrive via Lufthansa LH1710 (Munich → Dubrovnik) at 4:08 PM CEST
  • Take airport shuttle or taxi to hotel (30 min)
  • Check into hotel - Sun Gardens Dubrovnik in Orašac
  • Afternoon to explore at leisure

🏰 Enter Old Town

  • Pile Gate - Main western entrance to the walled city
  • Cross drawbridge into UNESCO World Heritage site
  • First glimpse of the stunning marble streets

🚶 Stradun (Placa)

  • Main street of Old Town - marble limestone polished by centuries of footsteps
  • Lined with baroque buildings rebuilt after 1667 earthquake
  • Cafes, shops, and constant buzz of activity

⛪ Old Town Highlights

  • Rector's Palace - Gothic-Renaissance palace, former seat of government
  • St. Blaise Church - Baroque masterpiece, patron saint of Dubrovnik
  • Onofrio's Fountain - 15th century water supply system
  • Sponza Palace - Gothic-Renaissance, one of few buildings to survive earthquake

🌅 Buža Bar

  • Cliffside bar built into city walls
  • Stunning sunset views over Adriatic Sea
  • Swim ladder for brave cliff jumpers
  • Perfect end to first Dubrovnik evening
Day 3 - Sunday, April 26
City Walls & Mount Srđ Hike

🏰 Walk the City Walls

  • 2km circuit around entire Old Town - UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • 1.5-2 hours to complete at leisurely pace
  • Start early (8 AM opening) to beat crowds and heat
  • Stunning views of terracotta rooftops, fortresses, and Adriatic
  • Entrance near Pile Gate or Ploče Gate
  • Bring water - limited shade and lots of stairs

🎬 Game of Thrones Locations (Optional)

  • Dubrovnik = King's Landing in the series
  • Walk of Shame steps near St. Ignatius Church
  • Fort Lovrijenac (Red Keep exterior)
  • Pile Gate (King's Landing entrance)
  • Guided tours available or self-explore

⛰️ Mount Srđ Hike

  • 412m elevation gain - moderate difficulty
  • 1 hour hike up serpentine trail (or take cable car)
  • Panoramic views - Best viewpoint over Dubrovnik Old Town and islands
  • See the entire walled city from above
  • Elaphiti Islands visible in distance

🏰 Fort Imperial

  • Napoleonic fortress at Mount Srđ summit
  • Croatian War of Independence Museum inside
  • 360-degree views of coastline
  • Sunset from here is spectacular

🍽️ Final Dinner

  • Fresh Adriatic seafood in Old Town
  • Try black risotto (cuttlefish ink)
  • Peka (meat/seafood under iron bell)
  • Croatian wines - Plavac Mali or Pošip
  • Celebrate an amazing Croatia journey!
Day 4 - Monday, April 27
Depart for Split

🚗 Pick Up Rental Car

  • Morning pickup at Dubrovnik City Center location
  • 7-day one-way rental to Zagreb
  • Check out of hotel and pack up

🛣️ Drive to Split

  • 3-hour scenic coastal drive (230km)
  • Follow D8 coast road or faster A1 motorway
  • Stunning Adriatic views along the way
  • Arrive Split early afternoon
  • Check into Boutique Hotel Luxe near Diocletian's Palace

🏛️ Split Exploration Begins

  • Afternoon free to start exploring Split
  • Walk through Diocletian's Palace
  • Dinner in the Old Town
💡 Dubrovnik Tips
Local Insights

City Walls Tips

  • Buy tickets in advance online to skip queues
  • Go counterclockwise (right from Pile entrance) - stairs easier this direction
  • Wear comfortable shoes - marble can be slippery
  • Start at 8 AM for best light and fewer people

Getting Around

  • Old Town is completely pedestrian-only
  • Hotels in Lapad (~2km away) cheaper than Old Town
  • Local buses frequent and cheap to Old Town
  • Walking everywhere in Old Town is easiest

Accommodation

  • Sun Gardens Dubrovnik - Luxury resort on the Adriatic coast
  • Stunning beachfront location with multiple pools and spa
  • 15 minutes from Old Town with complimentary shuttle service
  • Perfect blend of relaxation and access to city attractions

What to Eat

  • Seafood: Octopus salad, grilled fish, black risotto
  • Peka: Slow-cooked meat/seafood under iron bell
  • Rozata: Dubrovnik's version of crème caramel
  • Avoid restaurants on Stradun - overpriced and touristy
